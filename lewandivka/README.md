# Левандівка: по той бік району

**Lewandivka: The Other Side of the District** — a cooperative adventure for **Minecraft 1.20.1 (Fabric, Java 17)** for
**1, 2 or 3 players** (three is the intended experience, every puzzle and every boss scales down for two or one).

You get off the tram at the terminus of a grey panel-block district, build an abandoned kiosk, chase a Debtor, steal a
growling package out of Garage No. 13, survive the last tram, swallow *Chroma* — and cross over to **Chromandivka**: pink
sky, three suns, an unfinished rainbow ring, a Rainbow Garage Cooperative, a Shelter of Lost Names (two cats are
missing), a dry-lake Aquapark, a tram depot above the sky and, at the end of everything, a very grey tower with a very
grey Head who removed the colour "to remove chaos". First playthrough: about 4.5-6 hours. Ukrainian first (`uk_ua`), full
English translation (`en_us`).

> Everything in the repository is original: the code, 189 block textures, 42 item textures, 35 entity skins, models,
> animations, sounds and music are generated by the scripts in `tools/` (see `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`).
> The reference archive that came with the task was used as architectural inspiration only; no file from it is included.

## What you get

| | |
|---|---|
| **Worlds** | `lewandivka:district` (the real-world district, 300×300, deterministic, in the middle of an **endless open country** — forests, plains, rivers, lakes, seas, mountains, caves, ores, animals and the monsters of the night, a world to survive in like the ordinary overworld, with the story on top; as deep as the ordinary world: bedrock at y −64, deepslate below y 8, ores, caves and lava lakes under the streets too, gently rolling ground between the buildings and real hills outside) and `lewandivka:chromandivka` (floating islands, glowshroom basins, orange rivers, crystal shelves, five landmark dungeons and the tower). Both are produced by the mod's own chunk generator: quests can never be broken by terrain. |
| **The district's buildings** | Nothing is a painted box: the **panel blocks** have a lobby with letterboxes, a stairwell up to the roof and sixty **furnished flats** (beds, tables, sofas, kitchens, lamps, balconies); the **houses**, the **kindergarten**, the **school** (classrooms, a library, the hall), the **garages**, the **boiler house**, the transformer stations, the allotment sheds and the old shop all have rooms to walk through. More than seventy buildings in rows, courtyards and a private sector, a market, a football pitch and allotment gardens. The windows are tinted glass in white frames. **Furniture:** with the furniture mod **Handcrafted** (it is part of the pack) the beds, chairs, tables, sofas, shelves, cupboards, kitchen counters, ovens, cushions and crockery of the rooms are its models, and so are the park benches and the tables with chairs in the courtyards and behind the market stalls; without it the same pieces are built of the game's own blocks, so a world is complete either way. |
| **Cars and loot** | The cars of the streets and of the garage rows are **drivable cars of the mod Trep's Cars** (it is part of the pack; click a car to get in, W/A/S/D to drive). They are made by the mod when you come near them, once, and stay where you leave them; the cars of the district cannot be hurt, so a punch cannot blow one up. The cars of Trep's Cars exist where the mod can run, that is in a **singleplayer world or a world opened to LAN**; a **dedicated server cannot run that mod** (its code touches a class that only the game window has), so there the same cars stay built of blocks, as they have always been. **Every flat, house, the kindergarten, the school, every garage, shed and the old shop has chests and barrels with random loot** (food, torches, arrows, tools, books, seeds, and now and then an emerald, the money of the market); what is inside is made when a container is first opened, so no two playthroughs are the same. The pack also has **Alcohol Only** (vodka, beer, wine), **Fumee de Bushy** (tobacco, cigarettes) and **More Food** (pies, baguettes, sausage rolls, crisps, sweets ...): when they are installed their things turn up in the same containers (drinks and tobacco in the garages, the sheds, the shop and the flats, sweets and food in the kindergarten and the school; never a drink or a cigarette for the children's rooms). |
| **The people of the district** | Thirty **citizens** of eight kinds (an old woman, an old man, a worker, a student, a kid, a teacher, a yard keeper, a neighbour) stand about on the sidewalks, the plaza, the playground and the courtyards; right-click one and he says a few short words (five different things each, never the same twice in a row). Behind every stall of the **market** stands a **trader** (baker, greengrocer, butcher, handyman, flea trader, fishmonger, gardener): right-click opens the ordinary trade screen. They sell food and things (bread, pies, cooked meat and fish, torches, arrows, iron tools, a bed, a lead, seeds, saplings ...) for **emeralds** and buy the plain goods of a first day (cobblestone, coal, logs, wheat, potatoes, bones, string, fish ...) for emeralds, so the first emerald is a few minutes away. District tokens are not part of the trade: nothing a quest needs can be spent at the market. Their goods are made again every day; nobody can be hurt. |
| **Campaign** | 46 steps in 17 stages, saved in the world (data version 1), shared by the party, readable in the **District Notebook**. See `docs/QUEST_FLOW.md`. |
| **Dungeons** | Garage No. 13 · the Last Tram · the base and the coloured portal · Rainbow Garage · Shelter of Lost Names · Dry Lake Aquapark · sky ascent, the real 2.5 minute sky-tram ride and the Sky Depot · tower approach · Head of District Tower |
| **Bosses** | Garage King · Collar Collector · Lady Vortex · the Conductor · the Colorless Head (Chromatic Charge, three phases, 12 s overload, pass with **Q**) |
| **Abilities** | **Dash** (V), **Spring Insoles**, **Glider Ticket** (jump in mid-air). Server-validated, saved per player, caught up for late joiners. |
| **Co-op scaling** | The party size is read when an encounter starts. Fewer players: fewer adds, longer windows, lower boss health, relay devices instead of passing — never "wrong number of players". |
| **Safety nets** | Quest-item ledger (lost items are handed out again), checkpoints, `/lewandivka reset encounter`, boss wipe reset, falls put you back at a checkpoint. |
| **Accessibility** | Every telegraph uses at least two channels (text/shape + sound/colour); screen shake and flashes can be reduced or turned off; shaders are optional. |
| **Postgame** | Twelve cats of Lewandivka · where the seeds vanish · the wrong tram · Garage No. 0 · what was in the package. |

## Quick start with TLauncher (Windows)

1. Install [TLauncher](https://tlauncher.org) and start it **once** with any 1.20.1 version, then close it
   (this creates the `.minecraft` folder; TLauncher downloads Java 17 for you).
2. Download `Lewandivka-TLauncher-Installer-1.0.0.zip` from the release, unzip it and double-click **`install.bat`**
   (or right click `install.ps1` → *Run with PowerShell*). It
   * downloads the Fabric Loader and the listed mods from Modrinth (each file is hash-checked),
   * copies `lewandivka-1.0.0.jar` into `mods/`,
   * adds the graphics presets.
3. In TLauncher pick the version **`fabric-loader-0.16.10-1.20.1`** and press *Play*. (TLauncher also offers
   "Fabric 1.20.1" in its version list; then copy the jars from the installer's `mods` folder yourself.)
4. Choose the language *Українська* in *Options → Language* if you want the Ukrainian text (it is the default of the mod
   resources, the game language decides).

Linux / macOS: `./install.sh [game folder]` (needs `python3`, `curl`, `java`). Prism Launcher, MultiMC, ATLauncher or
the Modrinth App: import `Lewandivka-1.0.0.mrpack`.

**Playing together on TLauncher:** TLauncher accounts are offline accounts. Host a dedicated server with the **server pack**
(below, it already runs with `online-mode=false`) or open a singleplayer world to LAN. All players need the same mods —
use the same installer. The cars of Trep's Cars work only for the player who runs the game (the singleplayer world or the LAN host); on a dedicated server
they stay built of blocks, because that mod cannot start on a server.

## Server pack

`Lewandivka-Server-1.0.0.zip`: Fabric launcher, server mods only (no Sodium/Iris...), `server.properties` for 3 players,
`start.sh` / `start.bat` (they ask you to accept the Minecraft EULA). The jars of mods whose license does not let anybody pass
them on (Handcrafted) are not in the archive: `mods-download.txt` lists them and the start script fetches them from Modrinth the
first time (hash checked), so the first start needs internet access for that, too.

```text
java 17+  ->  ./start.sh   (or start.bat)        # first start downloads the vanilla server jar
```

Admin commands (permission level 2):

| Command | Does |
|---|---|
| `/lewandivka status` | stage, step, counters, rings, portal, bosses, party |
| `/lewandivka checkpoint` | teleport to the last checkpoint of the structure you stand in |
| `/lewandivka reset encounter <name>` | restart a dungeon (`garage13`, `tram_stop`, `rainbow_garage`, `shelter`, `aquapark`, `sky_ascent`, `sky_depot`, `tower_approach`, `tower`, `garage0`); add `full` to forget its progress too |
| `/lewandivka setstage <stage>` · `/lewandivka step <step>` | repair a stuck campaign |
| `/lewandivka party <1\|2\|3\|auto>` | force the scaling for tests |
| `/lewandivka ability grant\|revoke <player> <dash\|spring_insoles\|glider>` | abilities |
| `/lewandivka teleport <structure> [view]` | visit any structure of either dimension (tab completion lists them); `view` puts you on an aerial vantage point instead of the entrance |
| `/lewandivka teleport <structure> spot <name>` | to a named spot of a structure (a room, the roof, the place of a trader: `stall_0 vendor`, a citizen: `district citizen_3`) |
| `/lewandivka validate` | checks the generated structures against their blueprints |
| `/lewandivka decor` · `/lewandivka dumpblocks [namespace]` | which blocks the furniture of the rooms came out as (the blocks of the furniture mod or the game's own); the blocks of a mod with their properties |
| `/lewandivka cars` · `/lewandivka loot` · `/lewandivka dumpitems <namespace>` | makes the cars of Trep's Cars at every place of a car now (and takes away the old cars built of blocks of a world that was made before the mod was installed); rolls the table of every kind of container forty times and looks at containers in the world; lists the items and the creatures of a mod |
| `/lewandivka populace` | makes every citizen and every trader now (they appear by themselves when a player comes near) and says how many offers the traders have |
| `/lewandivka selftest` · `/lewandivka probe <x> <y1> <y2> <z>` | checks that need the running server: the generated chunks against the world plan, the first arrival, a fake player crossing between the dimensions, three players joining; and the blocks of a column (`execute in <dimension> run ...`) |
| `/lewandivka joinreplay start <label> <delay>` · `report` · `torture` · `result` · `/lewandivka trace <player> <ticks>` · `/lewandivka creatures` · `/lewandivka survey <x> <z> <radius>` · `/lewandivka sweep start <count> <side> <range>` · `structures <radius>` · `status` · `end` · `/lewandivka wild <x> <z> [view]` | developer diagnostics: joins fake players through the real join and decodes the packets a client would get, moves one around like the client test does; logs what the server knows about a real player on every tick; lists every creature of the mod in every world; counts the logs, water, ores, caves and animals around a point of the open country, generates squares of chunks of it and the structures of the game and lists the biomes and structures, stands (or hovers) there |

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| right click the **District Notebook** / **N** | open the notebook (quest, history, notes, abilities) |
| **H** | show the hint of the current step |
| **V** | Dash (after the Garage King) |
| **Jump in mid-air** | Glider (after the Conductor); jump again or land to stop |
| **Q** while you hold the Chromatic Charge | pass it to the player you look at (otherwise Q drops items as always) |

Settings: Mod Menu → *Lewandivka* (screen shake, reduced flashes, particles, boss difficulty, hints...) or
`config/lewandivka.json`.

## Graphics presets

`PERFORMANCE` (default: no shader, normal particles, 8-12 chunks) and `CINEMATIC` (MakeUp Ultra Fast shader, dynamic
lights, more particles, 12-16 chunks). Run `presets/apply_preset.bat CINEMATIC` (or `.sh`). Nothing in the game needs a shader.

## Troubleshooting

* **Crash "geckolib not found"**: install through the installer/mrpack, Fabric API and GeckoLib are separate mods (never
  inside `lewandivka.jar`).
* **Stuck?** `/lewandivka status`, then `/lewandivka reset encounter <name>` or `/lewandivka step <step>`.
* **Lost a quest item?** Wait two seconds: the ledger hands it out again (or stand still if your inventory is full).
* **Logs:** set `"developerLogs": true` in `config/lewandivka.json`.

## Building from source

```bash
./gradlew -p core test          # 280+ pure-Java tests of the campaign, flows, bosses, buildings and traders (no Minecraft needed)
./gradlew build                 # compiles against Minecraft 1.20.1 / Yarn, writes build/libs/lewandivka-1.0.0.jar
./gradlew runGametest           # Fabric GameTests on a headless server
bash tools/ci/smoke.sh          # boots a dedicated server and runs /lewandivka validate over rcon
python3 tools/validate_resources.py     # static checks of models, lang keys, sounds, data packs, ids used in code
python3 tools/generate_resources.py     # regenerates textures, models, animations, sounds, lang, data (see docs)
python3 tools/modpack/build_modpack.py --jar build/libs/lewandivka-1.0.0.jar --out dist   # needs internet
```

Continuous integration (`.github/workflows/lewandivka.yml`) runs all of this on every push, plus a real **client test**
(Xvfb + software OpenGL joins a server and takes screenshots of every structure) and boots the finished server pack.
