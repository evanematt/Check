Walkthrough template

A walkthrough in a modal. For every feature.

No demo mode, no real screens to drive. “Show me” turns the What’s new sheet into a short step-by-step walkthrough: a picture, a title, one sentence. New feature? Fill in the steps below. The design never changes.

Fixed, and what you fill in

Design decides the left once. A feature owner only writes the right.

Fixed

  • Same bottom sheet as “What’s new”
  • Step count, dots, Back and Next
  • Close (✕) on every step
  • Last step: Got it, plus an optional shortcut button

You fill in

  • One line for “What’s new”
  • Per step: a picture, a title, one sentence
  • Optional shortcut on the last step (e.g. “Open SIM settings”)
