Hand Portal

Portal: hold both hands up in an "L" shape. Your thumbs and index fingers frame a live window of shader effects. Pinch closed and reopen to switch effects.

Snap: snap your fingers and you crumble into pixels and vanish. Snap again to come back. Allow the microphone too, since it listens for the snap sound.

Tip: lean out of frame for a second after starting (or press B with nobody in view) so it learns what's behind you.
